== RuleX

Explainable AI company

* founded in 2007
* latest product released in 2025

Aims to enable anyone to make informed decisions with AI assistance

* especially in life sciences and healthcare
* also in industrial processes

=== Logic Learning Machine

Rule based model

* different than decision tree

Characteristics:

* overlapping rules
* rules are grown independently
** less dependent on first split
** generates different points of view of phenomenon
** enables more stable decision boundary points
* more meaningful decision confidence
