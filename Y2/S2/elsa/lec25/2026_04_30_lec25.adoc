== Budgeting

We need to turn general objectives into concrete operations

=== What is a budget?

It is a quantitative model for a predefined period (typically a year)

Need to ensure that we can meet objectives with resources in budget

The overall budget is a combination of smaller budgets

* HR budget
* RnD budget
* Care budget
* etc.

Budget components

. Quantified program
* define targets and resources
. Cause-effect model
* simulates impact of operations on costs
. Coherence tool
* aligns decisions made by different departments towards common target

=== Why budget?

* Decision coherence
** keep everyone on the same page
* Economic sustainability
** make sure we don't go broke
* Learning+control
** idk

=== Budget logic

Spending logic:

. Variables:
* operation volume
* unit costs and prices
* work hours
. Inputs:
* growth targets
* pricing policies
* quality standards
. Structural limits:
* capacity
* financial resources
* spending ceilings
. Relations:
* total cost
** unit price * quantity
* profit margin
** gross income - costs
* producton requirement

Not just spending logic:

* line-item budget
** see above
* global budget
** total spending limit
* program budget
** links resources to specific health goals
* performance-based
** rewards something???

3 main types:

==== Input based
* Personnel
* Drugs
* Physical goods

How many ibuprofens did we buy last year? Get 20% more

Also contains *line-item budgeting*

* individual items have costs
* historic costs affect new budgeting decisions
* does not take performance/output into account
** blind to results
* blindly/semi blindly increasing previous budgets can be inefficient

and *global budgeting*

* overall spending ceiling
* you have x dolans -> spend them however
** still have to spend reasonably
* more freedom for management
** can optimize more
* can also penalize quality to fit everything within the budget
** selecting cheaper patients to keep costs low

and also *peformance based budgeting*

* budgets are allocated according to performance targets and requirements
** e.g. correlate budget to outcome quality -> not easy
* requires low latency data to change budgeting logic
** integrating clinical/administrative data almost in real time
* different hospital departments have different complexities
** managing them is complex

==== Program budgeting

where tf do thees come out of

Resources are aggregated by health programs instead of administrative units

* vaccination campaigns
* oncology

Horizontal integration

* buget affects slices of treatment cycle
** follows patient

==== Output based
Focuses on performed activities

* How many visits did the clinic perform?

Volume-based resources

==== Outcome based
Similar to output

More focused on health outcomes

== Budgeting in healthcare

Economic sustainability isn't the only target

We want to ensure high quality care

* production planning
* coordination between hospital-territory
** avoiding bottlenecks, overworking certain facilities
* responsibilization of clinical staff
** operations, costs, quality

Budgeting links political vision to operational management

It has many roles specific to healthcare

* guaranteeing Minimum Assistance Levels
** responding to healthcare needs
* financial constraints
** dealing with regional/national spending budgets
** ensuring economical sustainability

Budget becomes a contract

* targets have resources
* targets have to be achieved using resources in some time frame

We can't pick single pieces

* need to integrate multiple budgeting logics
* some are more appropriate for some tasks

=== Management role

Managment has to deal with clinical staff and organizational processes

* balancing healthcare demands with other needs

They manage resources/targets

* balancing effectiveness with efficiency

They become responsible for targets
